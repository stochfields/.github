# StochFields — Stochastic analysis of quantum fields

StochFields explores stochastic analysis as a language for interacting
fields: constructing their laws, understanding their observables, and
connecting their behaviour across scales. The programme connects singular
stochastic PDEs, constructive quantum field theory, renormalisation,
geometry, noncommutative probability, and computation.

StochFields is based at the University of Oxford and supported by the
UKRI Horizon Europe Guarantee award *Stochastic analysis of quantum
fields*, [EP/Z534328/1](https://gtr.ukri.org/projects?ref=EP/Z534328/1)
(April 2025 – April 2030).

StochFields also works within the Simons Collaboration on
[Probabilistic Paths to Quantum Field Theory](https://probabilistic-qft.org/).
